Philosophy

Create more. Share less.

Anubhav is a social platform built around real-life experiences — what you create, learn, explore, build, and experience. Built to feel closer to a thoughtful journal than an attention-seeking network.

What Makes Anubhav Different

Designed with human intention from the ground up.

Real experiences

Share what you actually did, created, learned, or explored. Honest reflections without performance.

No popularity race

No public follower counts or public like counts. You only see calm heart indicators without numeric competition.

No algorithmic feed

Posts are shown purely chronologically. No recommendation engines or engagement loops deciding what you view.

Calm by design

No endless notifications, comment debates, streaks, or reels. A quiet digital space that respects your peace of mind.

Experience over performance.
Creation over consumption.
People over algorithms.
Meaning over metrics.

Built for Focused Reflection

Everything you need to capture your journey, nothing you don't.

Chronological Feed

Read reflections and photo updates in order, without algorithmic noise or ads.

Rich Profiles

Share what you are currently working on and meaningful milestones you have accomplished.

Automatic Image Optimization

Smart on-device compression and rotation ensures swift uploads and minimal bandwidth usage.

Clean 3-Tab Interface

Just Feed, Create, and Profile. Simple, intuitive, and natural to navigate with one hand.

How to Install the APK

Install Anubhav directly on your Android device in three quick steps.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Anubhav button to download Anubhav-LiveitShareit.v1.0.0.apk.

2

Allow Installation

Open your download notification. If prompted, enable “Install unknown apps” for your browser in Settings.

3

Open & Begin

Tap Install, then launch Anubhav to create your account and start sharing real experiences.
